Flagship · Arabic RAG + predictive models

UJ Advisor

An Arabic academic advising assistant for University of Jeddah students. It answers questions about regulations, study plans, courses, co-op training, clubs and faculty offices, and flags students who may need academic or wellbeing support.

How a question is answered
Normalize + autocorrect→ Decision router→ RAGSQLRisk / support modelsWeb search→ Evidence fusion→ Arabic answer + memory
  • Hybrid retrieval over ChromaDB: dense vectors plus BM25, merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and parent-document aggregation.
  • A router chooses between retrieval, SQL lookups on a student database, the predictive models, or a Tavily web-search fallback.
  • TabNet classifier predicts academic risk from attendance, quizzes, assignments, midterm and project grades.
  • An autoencoder analyses support factors such as sleep, stress, family support and financial stress.
  • Conversation memory handles follow-up questions.

Recommender systems · Learning to rank

News Recommendation with a Learning-to-Rank Hybrid

Replicates a published news recommender that blends content-based filtering with SVD collaborative filtering (0.7 CF + 0.3 CBF), applies it to a new dataset, then improves on it. The final model learns how to rank clicked articles above unclicked ones instead of relying on fixed blend weights.

From baseline to final model
Entity-aware CBF (TF-IDF + entities)+ SVD on implicit clicks→ Paper-style 0.7 / 0.3 hybrid→ + popularity & recency→ Learning-to-Rank (gradient boosting)
  • Ranker features: CF score, CBF score, popularity, recency, category and subcategory match, entity overlap and history length.
  • Class-weighted HistGradientBoosting handles the 4% click rate; train/validation split by impression to avoid leakage.
  • SVD tuned by grid search; models compared on ranking metrics rather than RMSE, since the real task is ranking.

Machine learning · Healthcare

Predicting Thyroid Disease

Classifies patients as hyperthyroid, hypothyroid or negative from clinical and lifestyle features. Most patients are negative, so the real challenge was catching the rare hyperthyroid cases without losing accuracy elsewhere.

  • Cleaned hormone measurements (TSH, T3, TT4, T4U, FTI, TBG), traced missing values to tests that were never taken, removed outliers and a constant column.
  • Compared Decision Tree, Logistic Regression and AdaBoost; tuned AdaBoost with GridSearchCV.
  • Used balanced and custom class weights to lift minority-class recall; chose models by F1 because of the imbalance.

Optimization · Operations research

Production Scheduling Optimization

A factory making power drills, precision screwdrivers, washing machines and microwave ovens has to decide which machine handles each product. Every choice changes processing time, setup time and cost. This project solves the assignment exactly with Integer Linear Programming and compares it with Simulated Annealing, a faster heuristic that searches for near-optimal answers.

Two solvers, one objective
Minimize processing + setup time + cost→ ILP: exact, guaranteed optimumSimulated Annealing: probabilistic search→ Compare quality, speed, scalability

Salon Management Database

A central database for running a beauty salon. It tracks customers, stylists and their specialities, services and prices, appointments, payments, retail products, suppliers, seasonal discounts and customer reviews, replacing scattered paper and spreadsheet records.

Design process
Requirements & entities→ ER diagram→ Functional dependencies→ 1NF → 2NF → 3NF→ Oracle schema + PL/SQL
  • Junction tables resolve the many-to-many links: appointments to services, payments to products, services to discounts, products to suppliers.
  • Stored procedures add appointments with sequence-generated IDs, update appointment status and look up customers, with error handling.
  • Seeded with sample data across every table to test the relationships.

Diet System

A desktop app that helps people plan meals around their calorie needs. Users sign up, calculate their daily intake from weight, height, age and sex with the Harris–Benedict equation, then build a day of meals and see whether they are over or under their target.

  • Sign-up with password confirmation, saved to a file.
  • Menu-driven navigation between the calorie calculator and meal planner.
  • Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free modes colour each food green or red so users see what fits their diet.
  • Each meal is its own class, keeping calorie logic and dietary rules together.
